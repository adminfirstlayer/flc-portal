How your production units work

Video on your plan comes as production units. You choose the mix of quick videos and bigger edits.

1 unitStandard video

Up to 30 seconds and up to 5 cuts. A UGC-style talking head, product demo or simple ad.

2 unitsPremium video

30 to 50 seconds, 6 to 13 cuts, heavier editing. Story-led ads, launch and brand videos.

1 unitHook variants

2 new hooks on a video you already have, so you can test what stops the scroll.

£200 each · extraExtended video

Over 50 seconds or more than 13 cuts. Added on top of your plan, uses no units.

A cut is each change of shot. If a video goes past the limits for its type, it moves up to the next type, and we'll always tell you first. Unused units don't roll over. Revisions and extra sizes don't use units.
