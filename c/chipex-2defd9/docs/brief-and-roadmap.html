02The goal

Show you, in two batches, that we can make honest, realistic repair content and studio-quality kit shots, with a fresh angle for each of your four buyers, so you go into the spring ramp-up with new creative ready to test instead of the same ads since August.

Context we're working to: your target CPA of £10 or less (never above £15), Pro is about 75% of sales, and winter is the quiet season, so this Pilot leans towards site visuals plus a handful of ads.

04Guardrails

  • BrandYour logo and guidelines (chipex.pdf from your form). Real kit contents and sizes exactly as you listed them (tube 170 × 100; paint, blend and polish bottles per kit).
  • Tone: it isStraightforward, honest, quality.
  • Tone: it isn'tFlippant, down market, a "casual quick fix". "No Essex boys", no bodge jobs, no slang captions.
  • Claims you can make"Excellent Trustpilot review score". Paint colour matched to your registration. Results range from flawless to hard to see, if done carefully. Wording for each claim to be signed off by you before anything runs.
  • Avoid"Invisible", "vanishes", "miracle" or 100% promises. Christmas gift angles. AI-looking stock scenes. Naming or knocking competitors. Rushing: the repair takes patience, and we show that.
